## Public Reach Evidence

Confirmed:

- TalkingTree was linked and recommended by Modern Hiker.
- "The Talking Tree" appeared in a broad photoblog directory.
- A TalkingTree-hosted image was externally cited by a Moapa Band of Paiutes presentation hosted on SlideServe.
- Later public coverage connected the Talking Tree identity to large audience reach on Instagram and Southwest photography.

Unconfirmed:

- subscriber counts for the original PixelPost site
- original RSS subscriber numbers
- original Photoblogs.org ranking
- original inbound link count
- original trackback count
- formal award ranking

## Strongest Footprint Evidence

The strongest external footprint evidence is the combination of:

1. Modern Hiker directly praising and linking the photoblog.
2. The Top Photoblogs directory listing "The Talking Tree" with `talkingtree.org`.
3. SlideServe preserving an external citation of a specific TalkingTree image URL.

Together these prove that the public web remembered the site as more than an isolated personal archive.

## Evidence Boundary

This document does not rely on local recovered exports, local image directories, or user-held artifacts. Those internal artifacts are covered in earlier archaeology phases. Phase 10 is limited to external corroboration.
