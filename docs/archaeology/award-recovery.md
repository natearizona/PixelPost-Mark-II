# Award Recovery

Phase: PixelPost Archaeology Phase 10 - Provenance & Award Recovery

Mode: read-only external corroboration. No artifacts were modified, imported, restored, committed, or pushed.

## Operating Question

Did TalkingTree receive any documented awards, nominations, rankings, or recognitions?

Answer: independent recognition is confirmed, but a formal Photobloggies, Photoblog Awards, Best Landscape Photoblog, Best Nature Photoblog, finalist, or honorable mention placement was not confirmed in this pass.

## Award Search Results

Searches did not confirm TalkingTree in:

- Photobloggies winners
- Photobloggies finalists
- Photoblog Awards winners
- Photoblog Awards finalists
- Best Landscape Photoblog award lists
- Best Nature Photoblog award lists
- formal honorable mention lists

This is a negative finding, not proof that no recognition ever existed. It means Phase 10 did not locate externally verifiable award placement.

## Award Context

The wider award ecosystem did exist during the relevant period. Search results surfaced external references to Photoblog Awards / Photoblogsawards.com and category-level awards, including Open Culture's summary of "The Best Photoblogs of 2007" and Barcelona Photobloggers' 2009 Photoblog Awards post:

- https://www.openculture.com/2008/01/the_best_photoblogs_of_2007.html
- https://barcelonaphotobloggers.org/2009/12/31/ganadores-de-los-photoblog-awards-2009/

Neither located source identified TalkingTree as a winner or finalist.

## Final Answer

No documented award, nomination, finalist placement, or honorable mention was confirmed. The strongest recognition evidence is third-party recommendation and directory inclusion, especially Modern Hiker and the Top Photoblogs directory page.
